# Touch-labor fact sheet: 10 candidate manufacturing locations

Prepared 2026-10-19. Fact-finding only; nothing here ranks or recommends a site. Full data, sources and
per-row citations are in `output/touch_labor_comparison.xlsx`.

**Two names were corrected, please confirm.** "Rocket Center, WY" is treated as **Rocket Center, WV**
(Mineral County; no such place exists in Wyoming). "Luka, MS" is treated as **Iuka, MS** (Tishomingo County).

**Touch labor** here means hands-on production work (assemblers, machinists, welders, inspectors) plus the
lower-skill hourly pool manufacturing recruits from or loses workers to (retail, warehouse and distribution,
material movers).

## What the data shows

**Population and labor supply.** Only Baltimore, San Diego, Palmdale, Sunnyvale and Redondo Beach have large
resident labor pools inside the city itself. Clearfield (34K) and Corinne (783 people) draw from the Wasatch Front
(Davis, Weber, Cache, Box Elder), about 0.8 to 1.8 million people. Rocket Center and Iuka are rural: 27K and 19K
in their counties, with commute sheds of roughly 360K to 390K that cross state lines (WV/MD/PA and MS/AL/TN).

**Education.** The share of adults with a high school diploma or less ranges from 26% (Sunnyvale) to 51% (Rocket
Center); Iuka is 50%. Bachelor's+ runs from 21% (Iuka) to 52% (Sunnyvale). The nearest 2-year and trade schools within
30 miles: 101 around Redondo Beach, 30 San Diego, 25 Baltimore, 22 Sunnyvale, 11 Clearfield, 9 Palmdale, 5 each at
Corinne and St. Augustine, 4 at Iuka and 3 at Rocket Center (public community colleges: 0 near Corinne, 1 near
Clearfield and St. Augustine, 2 near Rocket Center and Palmdale, 3 near Iuka).

**Wages.** Production medians cluster at $22 to $24/hr in most places, $27.13 in Sunnyvale and $19.65 in the Iuka
area. Entry-level hourly pay for movers and retail is lowest in Iuka ($16.72 and $13.39) and Rocket Center ($17.60
and $13.93). Corinne's Walmart distribution center was seen advertising freight-handler pay of $24.20 to $27.70/hr,
above the area's $23.99 production median (job-posting evidence, not survey data).

**Existing manufacturing concentration.** Production-job location quotients are highest in the Iuka area (2.49),
Clearfield/Ogden (1.39) and Corinne's area (1.32); lowest in Baltimore (0.42), St. Augustine/Jacksonville (0.57)
and Sunnyvale (0.71). A high quotient means a trained workforce but also more competitors.

URLs, confidence and caveats):

- **Sunnyvale:** Lockheed Space (3,362), Intuitive Surgical (5,165), Applied Materials (2,919), Northrop (1,637); Tesla Fremont (23,000) at the edge of the shed. No warehouse employers inside the city.

What stands out: hourly hiring is dominated by retail and dollar-store chains at every site, so manufacturing
competes for the same entry-level pool mostly on pay and schedule. Production-type postings are concentrated at
Redondo Beach (SpaceX and Relativity), Clearfield (Williams International, Northrop) and Corinne (Campbell's, West
Liberty, Northrop Promontory). Rocket Center and St. Augustine show very little visible hourly demand. Posted pay
is usually not stated (a site median is shown only with 10+ priced postings: about $22/hr in most CA and UT
sites, $18.50 in Baltimore, $21 in the Iuka area).

Limits: postings are demand signals, not headcount (one posting can mean several hires; evergreen postings
persist); only covered employers appear (FedEx, Tesla, General Atomics, Publix, hospitals and most staffing firms
could not be read without bypassing bot protection or logins); Amazon's hourly fulfillment roles sit on a separate
site that is not read; Dollar General figures are per-store postings; job-title classification is keyword-based.

## Caveats

- Small-place ACS figures (Corinne, Iuka) carry wide margins of error; Rocket Center has no city-level data.
- OEWS wages are regional. Palmdale and Redondo Beach share the Los Angeles area figure.
- The employer list is researched, not exhaustive. Several counts are multi-site, statewide, projected or old (flagged in the workbook notes), including the Boeing, Raytheon and 2019 San Diego rows.
- The labor shed is straight-line distance, not commute time or actual commuting flows.
- County Business Patterns counts (workbook sheet "Large estabs (CBP)") are zero when no site that size exists or the cell is suppressed.
- Not covered: housing availability, incentives, utilities and land, union density, or turnover and attrition.
