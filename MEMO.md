# Touch-labor fact sheet: 10 candidate manufacturing locations

Refreshed 2026-10-26 (first prepared 2026-10-19). Fact-finding only; nothing here ranks or recommends a site. Full
data, sources and per-row citations are in `output/touch_labor_comparison.xlsx`.

**Two names were corrected, please confirm.** "Rocket Center, WY" is treated as **Rocket Center, WV**
(Mineral County; no such place exists in Wyoming). "Luka, MS" is treated as **Iuka, MS** (Tishomingo County).

**Touch labor** here means hands-on production work (assemblers, machinists, welders, inspectors) plus the
lower-skill hourly pool manufacturing recruits from or loses workers to (retail, warehouse and distribution,
material movers).

## What changed in this refresh

- **Population, education, wages:** unchanged. ACS 2020-2024 and BLS OEWS May 2025 are still the newest releases
  (the next OEWS is due around spring 2027).
- **Updated sources:** County Business Patterns 2022 to 2023, the IPEDS college directory 2022 to 2025, and the Census
  gazetteer 2023 to 2025. Community colleges that now award a few bachelor's degrees (Antelope Valley College, College
  of the Canyons) are counted as 2-year again, so school counts no longer drop for them.
- **Employers re-researched** against 2025-2026 sources. Biggest moves: Northrop Space Park (Redondo Beach) 9,396 to
  6,845; Northrop Clearfield 1,000-1,999 to 2,000-2,999; General Atomics 15,000 (2019, company-wide) to about 10,800;
  Qualcomm San Diego 12,794 to 10,073 (the old figure belonged to the school district); Anduril's Baltimore shipyard
  about 3,000 to 3,100 jobs (announced 2026, funding still needs approval); the Amazon St. Augustine delivery station
  was never built and is removed; Lakeshore Learning's Garland DC has opened.
- **Job postings:** re-pulled from all 97 company boards. Of 10,409 active postings, 1,216 are new and 1,326 closed
  since 2026-10-19, roughly 12% weekly turnover (see below).

## What the data shows

**Population and labor supply.** Only Baltimore, San Diego, Palmdale, Sunnyvale and Redondo Beach have large
resident labor pools inside the city itself. Clearfield (34K) and Corinne (783 people) draw from the Wasatch Front
(Davis, Weber, Cache, Box Elder), about 0.8 to 1.8 million people. Rocket Center and Iuka are rural: 27K and 19K
in their counties, with commute sheds of roughly 360K to 390K that cross state lines (WV/MD/PA and MS/AL/TN).

**Education.** The share of adults with a high school diploma or less ranges from 26% (Sunnyvale) to 51% (Rocket
Center); Iuka is 50%. Bachelor's+ runs from 21% (Iuka) to 52% (Sunnyvale). The nearest 2-year and trade schools within
30 miles: 100 around Redondo Beach, 35 San Diego, 26 Baltimore, 23 Sunnyvale, 10 Clearfield, 9 Palmdale, 5 at St.
Augustine, 4 each at Corinne and Iuka and 3 at Rocket Center (public community colleges: 22 Redondo Beach, 14
Sunnyvale, 8 San Diego, 6 Baltimore, 3 each at Palmdale and Iuka, 2 at Rocket Center, 1 each at Clearfield and St.
Augustine, 0 near Corinne).

**Wages.** Production medians cluster at $22 to $24/hr in most places, $27.13 in Sunnyvale and $19.65 in the Iuka
area. Entry-level hourly pay for movers and retail is lowest in Iuka ($16.72 and $13.39) and Rocket Center ($17.60
and $13.93). Corinne's Walmart distribution center was seen advertising freight-handler pay of $24.20 to $27.70/hr,
above the area's $23.99 production median (job-posting evidence from 2026-10-19, not survey data).

**Existing manufacturing concentration.** Production-job location quotients are highest in the Iuka area (2.49),
Clearfield/Ogden (1.39) and Corinne's area (1.32); lowest in Baltimore (0.42), St. Augustine/Jacksonville (0.57)
and Sunnyvale (0.71). A high quotient means a trained workforce but also more competitors.

**Large employers already in the labor pool** (headcounts from the cited source and year; see the workbook for
URLs, confidence and caveats):

- **Palmdale:** Northrop Grumman (9,476) and Lockheed Martin (4,151) at Plant 42, and Northrop has broken ground on a new B-21 assembly line there; Walmart (1,199), Palmdale Regional Medical Center (1,081), a distribution firm (681).

What stands out: hourly hiring is dominated by retail and dollar-store chains at every site, so manufacturing
competes for the same entry-level pool mostly on pay and schedule. Production-type postings are concentrated at
Redondo Beach (SpaceX and Relativity), Clearfield (Williams International, Northrop) and Corinne (Campbell's, West
Liberty, Northrop Promontory). Rocket Center and St. Augustine show very little visible hourly demand. Posted pay
is usually not stated (a site median is shown only with 10+ priced postings: about $22/hr in most CA and UT
sites, $19.50 in Baltimore, $21 in the Iuka area).

**Week over week (2026-10-19 to 2026-10-26):** 1,216 postings opened and 1,326 closed against 10,409 still open. San
Diego (+278 new), Sunnyvale (+362) and Redondo Beach (+246) had the most new postings. Walmart closed more than it
opened (181 closed, 73 new). Boards hosted on Eightfold (Qualcomm 161 new and 163 closed, Lam Research 56 and 56,
Lockheed Martin 119 and 112) show near-total turnover in one week, which may partly reflect re-posting or
renumbering at the source rather than real hiring changes, so treat their new/closed counts with care.

Limits: postings are demand signals, not headcount (one posting can mean several hires; evergreen postings
persist); only covered employers appear (FedEx, Tesla, General Atomics, Publix, hospitals and most staffing firms
could not be read without bypassing bot protection or logins); Amazon's hourly fulfillment roles sit on a separate
site that is not read; Walmart's search could not read Union City, CA; Dollar General figures are per-store
postings; job-title classification is keyword-based.

## Caveats

- Small-place ACS figures (Corinne, Iuka) carry wide margins of error; Rocket Center has no city-level data.
- OEWS wages are regional. Palmdale and Redondo Beach share the Los Angeles area figure.
- The employer list is researched, not exhaustive. Several counts are multi-site, statewide, projected or old (flagged in the workbook notes), including the Boeing and Raytheon El Segundo rows and the 2019 NASSCO and Northrop San Diego figures. Mineral County WV employers have 2013 rankings only.
- The labor shed is straight-line distance, not commute time or actual commuting flows.
- County Business Patterns counts (workbook sheet "Large estabs (CBP)", now 2023) are zero when no site that size exists or the cell is suppressed.
- Not covered: housing availability, incentives, utilities and land, union density, or turnover and attrition.
